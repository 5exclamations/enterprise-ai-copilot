# Warranty Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-15
Version: 1.8

## Coverage periods
Tools carry a 2 year warranty. Safety gear carries a 1 year warranty. Fasteners are guaranteed to meet specification for 90 days.

## Claims
Warranty claims require proof of purchase. Misuse, modification and normal wear are excluded.

## Calibration
Torque wrenches must have their calibration certificate renewed annually to keep the warranty valid.
