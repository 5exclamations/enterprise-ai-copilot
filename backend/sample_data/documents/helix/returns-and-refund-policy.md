# Returns and Refund Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-15
Version: 3.2

## Return window
Customers may return standard products within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unopened and in original packaging. A Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number is required before any return is shipped.

## Restocking fee
Non-defective returns of standard items are charged a 15% restocking fee. The fee is waived for defective or incorrectly shipped items.

## Defective products
Defects must be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos. Helix will replace the item or issue a full refund and pays return shipping for defective items.

## Non-returnable items
Chemical products (SKUs starting with CH-) cannot be returned once opened. Custom or special-order items are non-returnable.

## Refund timing
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the returned goods are received and inspected.
