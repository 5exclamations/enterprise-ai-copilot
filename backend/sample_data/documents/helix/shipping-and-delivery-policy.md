# Shipping and Delivery Policy

Effective date: 2026-02-01
Version: 2.4

## Methods and cost
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and costs a flat $25. Standard shipping is free on orders where goods total $500 or more after discounts. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs a flat $60.

## Cutoff times
Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern Time ship the same business day. We do not deliver on weekends. International shipping is not supported.

## Hazardous materials
Chemical products (CH- SKUs) are hazmat. They ship by ground only and cannot be sent express. A hazmat surcharge of $35 applies per shipment.

## Shipping damage
Damage in transit must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos. Claims after 48 hours are not accepted.
