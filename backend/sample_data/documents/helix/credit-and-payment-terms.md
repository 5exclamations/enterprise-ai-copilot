# Credit and Payment Terms

Effective date: 2026-01-01
Version: 4.0

## Payment terms
Approved accounts have Net 30 payment terms. Customers who pay within 10 days receive a 2% early payment discount (2/10 net 30). New customers must prepay their first order.

## Credit limits
The standard credit limit is $10,000. Gold tier customers have a $25,000 limit and platinum tier customers have a $50,000 limit.

## Late payment
Late invoices incur a late fee of 1.5% per month. Orders are placed on hold when an account is more than 15 days past due.

## Accepted payment methods
We accept ACH, wire transfer and credit card. Credit card payments carry a 3% surcharge.
