# Data Privacy and Security Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01
Version: 2.1

## Access
Customer contact details are available to managers and admins only. Viewers see masked contact data.

## Credentials
API keys are rotated every 90 days. Never paste customer data or card numbers into external tools.

## Retention and incidents
Order records are retained for 7 years. Security incidents must be reported within 24 hours.
