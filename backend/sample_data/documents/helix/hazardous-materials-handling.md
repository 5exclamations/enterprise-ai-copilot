# Hazardous Materials Handling

Effective date: 2026-02-15
Version: 1.2

## Storage
Chemical products must be stored in flammables cabinets at both warehouses. Every chemical SKU must have a current Safety Data Sheet (SDS) on file.

## Training
Staff handling hazmat complete training every year.

## Spills
Any spill must be reported to the safety officer within 1 hour.
