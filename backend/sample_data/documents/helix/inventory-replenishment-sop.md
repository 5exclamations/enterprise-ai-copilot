# Inventory Replenishment SOP

Effective date: 2026-03-01
Version: 2.0

## Reorder trigger
Create a purchase order when available stock (on hand minus reserved) is at or below the product reorder point.

## Approval
Purchase orders over $5,000 require director approval. Purchase orders move from Draft to Manager approval to Sent.

## Lead times
Typical supplier lead times are 14 days for fasteners, 21 days for tools and 7 days for packaging.

## Stock adjustments and counts
Inventory adjustments of more than 100 units need manager approval and a written reason. Cycle counts are performed weekly for high-value items and monthly for all others. Discontinued SKUs are liquidated, not reordered.
