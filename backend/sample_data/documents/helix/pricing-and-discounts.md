# Pricing and Discounts

Effective date: 2026-01-01
Version: 1.5

## Tier discounts
Gold tier customers receive 5% off list price. Platinum tier customers receive 10% off list price. Standard tier customers pay list price.

## Volume discount
Orders with a pre-discount subtotal of $2,000 or more receive an additional 2% volume discount.

## Tax
Sales tax of 8% is applied to the discounted goods value. Shipping is not taxed.
